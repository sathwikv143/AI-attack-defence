    How to use

    1. Click a stage (blue) to list its attacks. Opening one stage closes another.
    2. Click an attack (red) to see Attack + Defences.
    3. Click an end node (filled circle) to open its note and Resources. Esc closes.
    4. Use Search to jump. Toggle Stages to scan by lifecycle column.

    Hollow circle = more inside. Filled circle = end of the branch.

    Fit clears pan/zoom · Reset returns to Training.
